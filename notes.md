# Notes

The tasks each day take longer and longer, going home every day is darker and darker, until sunsets and midnights, sunrise, lost sleep rythm, sleep deprived.

Deterioation effects are per-year, but can happen further into the year as the stress increases.

As years go on, walls start becoming more and more unstable. The backrooms are actually just the edge of the simulation becoming more and more repeated, more and more open, until it narrows down again into another location which start becoming more and more localized.

Its the simulated conciousness which is breaking down, not the simulation. It escapes through cognitive dissonance into another world, a world beyond just shutting down into a panic attack.

The end result is a gameplay arc that transforms a normal office into a landscape of progressive psychological surrealism, delivering both uneasy familiarity and terrifying unreality in equal measure.

## Stress effects buildup

Slight blurryness moment.

Mild migraine patch fading in.

Dizzyness and blurryness moment after getting up from desk and walking a bit. Movement has brownian velocity to have a "drunk" effect.

Forgetfulness. Computer mouse is missing. Has to walk around and look for it, only to find it in its place on the desk.

Temporary audio distortions.
Temporary deafness. Tinnitus.
Temporary tremmor and shaking.
Slower movement.
Temporary immovability.
Temporary color shift.
Temporary DOF.
Temporary blindness.

Numbness. Player can barely type and move mouse.
Slowly moving away from the screen until it is quite far away, but still interactible.

Losing balance. Stops moving, and has to  keep the character balanced with the mouse.

Auditory Echoes. Elevator ding heard multiple times, lagging effect on the birds, pc boot sound seems to be going in a loop.

Auditory hallucinations. Sounds getting swapped, like the elevator door opening sound playing when starting the computer. Phoen ringing elsewhere, footsteps, office chair rolling. Small sounds increase in loudness. Wall clock ticks louder, elevator more aggresive (deep drone fades up through the year, replacing the mechanical sound). Boot sound plays backward.

Intrusive afterimages. When looking at white, computer interface (pixelated CRT) slowly fades in, until looked away. Essentially, the whiter the pixel, the faster the image fades in, until it fades away by rapid color change, e.g. looking another direction quickly.

Hallucinations. Seeing double cursors, things outside the window, the screen emmissive material UVs start to move out of the screen bounds. Text on screen jumples like dyslexia. Text distort, blur, rearrange. Things switch place with a flicker, not shifting layout to make more seamless. Emails have momentary swear words, switched in with normal words quickly after. Large objects take the shape of a human when viewed from afar. Reflections in the monitor; door closing, light turning off, so on, but not really happening.

Sleep deprivation. Shadowy figures appear to creep in from the edge of vision.

Dissasotiation. If player walks in a straight line for a while, they start to slow down and get muffled sound.

Stroke. Camera rolling, drooping, and inputs slowly rotate interpolating to different axis.

Tunnel vision, desaturation.

Panic attack. Increasing intensity, starts hugging the wall, movement slowing down, sitting down closer to floor, immovability, head down, having to fight blackout.

Player acts on their own. Clinging to wall with window. Walking towards the window in their final moments, seemingly to look at the birds, but instead leaps out the building.

## Deterioation

Printer has “0 documents queued” but continues printing pages.

Rooms switch places. Player is forced to explore. Hallways stretch longer than before, a familiar route loops in circles. Objects move slightly when not looked at. Open a door, and for one second it leads to a different location before its the office again.

Security cameras. Paranoia. Later, cameras start following, but stop moving when looked at. Tiny motor whirring sound is unsettling. Noise is amplified with auditorial hallucinations with the stress effects.

Post it notes appear on monitor.

Tasks requires completion twice randomly.

Ceiling moving closer. Ceiling having slow animated wavy offset effect.

Insects.

Computer is in an infinite boot loop.

Faint visual tears at the edge of the screen; a “developer console” briefly opens and closes; typing on keyboard auto-fills commands the player didn’t type.

World goes upside-down. World rotates around slowly. Select objects react to the gravity change with solid body physics simulation.

Fly keeps landing on screen.

Reflections of object is reversed, making the player walk past themselves when walking along reflective surface.

office lights begin strobing rapidly, an alarm siren blares at maximum volume, the computer screens flash bizarre images or error messages, and the room sways as if the building itself is unstable. This barrage of stimuli bombards the player from all angles.
